01

Product overview

Click&Play is a two-sided marketplace: sports clubs list their courts and coaches; players discover clubs and self-book time slots online. It spans 11 racket/team sports, but tennis is the anchor.

The product is organised around three nouns — clubs, coaches and players — mirrored directly in the top navigation (Клубове / Играчи / Новини plus Създай своя клуб = "Create your club"). Everything a guest can do funnels toward one action: opening a club's daily schedule and clicking a free slot to reserve.

◎Discovery

Browse clubs by sport, filter by city (26) and payment type. Each sport has its own landing page and club count.

▦Booking

A live courts × hours grid per club, per day. Click a free cell → modal → confirm. Duration and payment method chosen inline.

✦Community

Public directories of players and coaches, each with a profile. A privacy toggle controls whether a player is listed.

Business modelThe platform is a SaaS + marketplace for clubs. Clubs self-register, configure courts/pricing/coaches, then are manually activated by an admin. Revenue is almost certainly club subscriptions and/or a cut of online payments — the public site shows sponsor tie-ins (Inbet, DEVIN Active) as an additional channel.

Supported sports

🎾 Tennis (71)🏓 Table tennis (14)🏸 Badminton (2) 🎾 Padel (17)⚽ Football (20)🏀 Basketball (6) 🏐 Volleyball (4)🏖 Beach tennis (2)🏐 Beach volleyball (2) 🥒 Pickleball (5)🎾 Squash (1)

Each sport is a first-class filter with its own route (/home/sport/s-tennis, s-padel, …). Club counts are live and should be derived, not stored.

Slot states

The live legend defines seven states, each with its own colour: Свободно (free), Резервирано (reserved), Моите резервации (my reservations), Не е налично (unavailable), Събитие (event), Турнир (tournament), and past hours ("old-hour", greyed). Pricing varies by time of day — daytime slots at 14€, evening (17:00+) at 16€.

The reservation modal — real payload

Clicking a free cell calls showPop("reservationModal", {…}). The exact object captured from the live site — this is your booking API contract:

// onclick payload of a free slot (360 Тенис клуб, court 706)
{
  "court": "Централен, Клей, 360 Тенис клуб",
  "club_id": 100,          "court_id": 706,
  "price": 14,             "date_time": 1788526800,
  "date_time_str": "04.09.2026 16:00:00",
  "hour": "16:00",        "end_duration": "16:00",
  "duration": "60 мин ( до 17:00 ч.)",
  "payment": "На място",   // on-site
  "min_reservation_time": 60,
  "allow_halfs": 0,        "half_data": [],
  "coaches": null,         "selectedCoach": 0,
  "is_closed": 0, "busy": null, "reservation": 0
}

Modal fields shown to the user

  • Court — name + surface + club (read-only)
  • Date & hour — read-only, from the clicked cell
  • Duration <select end_duration> — options generated from contiguous free slots (e.g. 60 / 120 min)
  • Payment method <select payment_method> — per-club subset of {on-site, online, free, multisport}
  • Amount — recalculated from duration × slot price
  • Reserve — POST → /reservation/court_reservation
Critical rebuild logicTwo rules must be enforced server-side, never trusted from the client: (1) slot locking / no double-booking — concurrent clicks on the same cell need a transactional guard; (2) duration validity — a 120-min booking requires the next cell also free. Price is recomputed from the source of truth on submit. The public payload is a convenience, not authorization.
05

Coach discovery & booking

Coaches are attached to clubs and have their own bookable calendar — the platform monetises lessons, not just court time. This is the flow you specifically called out.

Open a club, go to the "Треньори" tab

Each club page has four tabs: About · Daily · Weekly · Coaches. The coaches tab lists each trainer as an initials-avatar card with a short bio and "Виж профил".

/{slug}-view-coaches{id}.html

View the coach profile

Opens the coach's page titled "График на {name}" — a weekly availability grid (7 days × 24 hours) using the same slot states as courts. Empty cells = not offered; free cells = bookable lesson times.

/coaches/view/{coachId}

Select a free lesson slot

Clicking a free slot triggers the same reservation modal. The booking payload carries coaches and selectedCoach fields, so a court slot can also be booked with a coach attached.

Confirm → reservation with coach_id

The resulting reservation links player + court/time + coach. "Free play" bookings simply leave coach_id null.

Data insightBecause the slot payload already contains a coaches array, coach availability is modelled as an overlay on court/time, not a separate silo. A rebuild should treat "book a coach" and "book a court" as one reservation flow with an optional coach dimension — this keeps pricing, conflicts and the calendar unified. Not every club has coaches (many show "Няма регистрирани треньори" = none registered), so it must be optional per club.
06

Roles & authentication

Three roles: guest, player (member), and club/admin. Auth is classic email + password with reCAPTCHA; no social login was observed (only a Facebook page link).

👤Player registration

POST /auth/postRegister

  • name (Име и фамилия) *
  • email *
  • password + confirmation *
  • g-recaptcha-response * (Google reCAPTCHA)
  • terms checkbox *
  • subscribe — marketing opt-in
  • is_show — list me in the player directory

🔑Login & sessions

/auth/index — email + password. Session-based (server-rendered). Language preference (/home/language/*) is also session-scoped.

Login-gated actions: submitting a reservation, "My reservations" state in the grid, and any member dashboard (not visible to guests — see §11).

Rebuild recommendationKeep email+password but add OAuth (Google/Apple) and swap reCAPTCHA for a privacy-friendlier challenge (hCaptcha/Turnstile). The is_show flag is a real GDPR-relevant consent — model it explicitly and default to off.
07

Club onboarding (B2B)

The supply side. Clubs self-register through a single form that creates both the club record and the owner's account, then wait for manual activation.

🏛Join request form

POST /clubs/post_join_request

  • club_name, club_city, phone
  • name (owner), email, password + confirm
  • terms, reCAPTCHA

⚙Post-signup (inferred)

On-screen copy: "fill in all club & court info, then contact the admin to activate." This implies a club admin panel (behind login) to configure courts, surfaces, lighting, pricing, opening hours, coaches, and payment methods — plus a manual approval gate.

Biggest hidden surfaceThe club admin back-office is not publicly visible but is the largest build item: court/schedule editor, dynamic pricing (time-of-day, seasonal), coach management, reservation calendar/overrides, events & tournaments, payouts. Budget for it explicitly — it dwarfs the public booking UI.

Observed tech & recommended rebuild stack

What the current site appears to run on, and a pragmatic modern stack for the rebuild.

Observed on the live site

  • Server-rendered, /controller/action routing + slugged .html URLs — classic PHP MVC (CodeIgniter/Laravel-style) signature.
  • jQuery-era front end — inline onclick handlers, a global showPop() modal helper, full-page reloads for date/tab changes.
  • Google reCAPTCHA and Google Maps embeds.
  • Multi-language via session route switch; some mixed BG/RU strings in filters (i18n debt).

Recommended rebuild

LayerRecommendationWhy
Front endNext.js / React (SSR)Keep SEO from SSR; make the grid a live, no-reload component
APIREST/GraphQL over Node or LaravelClean booking contract; reuse team's language
DBPostgreSQLTransactional slot locking; millions of reservation rows
RealtimeWebSocket/SSE on the gridLive "just booked" updates prevent collisions
PaymentsStripe + local (myPOS) + MultiSportOnline payment is already a filter; formalise it
Search/i18nManaged locale files (BG/EN/RU)Fix mixed-language strings observed
Maps / captchaGoogle Maps · Turnstile/hCaptchaSame UX, better privacy

Gaps & things to verify

This audit covers the guest-visible product only. To finish the spec, the following need a logged-in account or club access to confirm.

  • Member dashboard — "My reservations", cancellation window/policy, profile editing, match history. Grid shows a "Моите резервации" state, implying it exists.
  • Club admin back-office — the entire supply-side UI (§7). Highest-effort unknown.
  • Online payment flow — the filter offers "online payment"; the one club sampled only exposed "on-site". Confirm the card/checkout UX and provider.
  • MultiSport / corporate cards — how acceptance maps to booking & settlement.
  • Events & tournaments — slot states exist for both; the creation/registration flow is unseen.
  • Cancellation, no-show, refund rules — business policy, not visible publicly.
  • Notifications — booking confirmation emails/SMS (subscribe flag hints at email infra).
  • Mobile app — verify whether a native app exists alongside the responsive web.
